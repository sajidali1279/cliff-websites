Admin Platform

What the people running the store actually see

Lucky Stop's admin isn't a settings page bolted onto an app. It's the operational core for 12+ stores. Hover to look inside.

Inventory Management

Every SKU tracked from request to restock.

  • Scanned-product catalog with a manual "Add Product" fallback for anything without a barcode
  • Per-store Order Lists with a Quick Add panel for fast reordering
  • Auto-reopens on close, plus a standing per-store instructions field so nothing falls through the cracks
Employee & Access Management

Role-based access, enforced at the API. Not just hidden UI.

  • 5-level role hierarchy: DevAdmin, SuperAdmin, Store Manager, Employee, Customer
  • Every permission checked server-side, not just hidden in the interface
  • Weekly shift scheduling with employee-submitted shift-change requests
Rates & Cashback Engine

5 loyalty tiers, per-category bonuses, live-configurable.

  • Bronze through Diamond tiers, each with its own base cashback rate
  • Category bonus rates stack on top: groceries, hot foods, tobacco, and more
  • Optional flat cents-per-gallon mode for gas and diesel instead of a percentage
Analytics & Reporting

Daily numbers, broken down by store.

  • Daily transaction and revenue charts, including platform-fee breakdown
  • Inventory Intelligence: most-ordered items, by category, by store
  • Auto-generated monthly invoices per store
Fraud Protection & Audit Trail

Every grant, edit, and override is logged.

  • Receipt photo mandatory before any points grant
  • Full activity log across point grants, offer edits, staff and schedule changes
  • Customer-initiated disputes resolved against the receipt on file
Notices & Alerts

One pinned message, seen by every store instantly.

  • Admin-authored notices pinned to every employee's chat
  • Push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) across iOS, Android, and web
  • Deep linking: tap a notification about a dispute, a request, or a schedule change and land on that exact item, not just the home screen
  • Built-in support chat between store staff and HQ

About

"Local businesses deserve the same quality of technology that large chains have not templates, but complete platforms."

Cliff Industries builds software for local and regional businesses. We focus on retail, automotive, hospitality, and operations the kinds of businesses that are the backbone of every community but rarely get enterprise-quality tools.

Every product is built end-to-end: the mobile app, the web dashboard, the backend API, the database, and the cloud infrastructure. No handoffs, no gaps.

Based in Texas, USA. Named for the cliff and the Indus: elevation and flow, structure and ambition.
